## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet, masculine luxury and tactile precision for an exclusive European boutique specializing in high-grade men’s underwear and swimwear (featuring MANSTORE, Olaf Benz, and CALIDA). 

The aesthetic is built on high-fashion editorial rigor: razor-sharp zero-radius geometry, generous organic whitespace, and an uncompromising pairing of haute-couture display serifs with highly functional, clean modern sans-serif body typography. The visual language rejects gratuitous dropshadows, loud gradients, and playful roundness. Instead, it mirrors architectural minimalism, museum catalogues, and luxury print publishing: quiet confidence, tactile organic tones, sharp discipline, and unhurried visual rhythm.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, warm-toned foundation inspired by fine cotton, linen paper, and architectural foliage:

- **Primary (`#1F4A39` / Brand Deep `#183F30`)**: Deep evergreen slate, representing permanence, luxury, and understated authority. Used for top announcement bars, primary interactive buttons, and primary focal points.
- **Secondary (`#626960`)**: Muted olive-slate for secondary typography, metadata, specs, and understated icons.
- **Tertiary (`#DCDED5`)**: Quiet warm grey-green for hairline dividers, precision card borders, and unselected variant strokes.
- **Neutral (`#171E19`)**: Deep near-black, ensuring high-contrast legibility for headings and primary pricing without the harshness of pure black.
- **Page Background (`#F8F8F3`)**: Warm off-white milk tone that provides an inviting, non-glare surface for imagery.
- **Surface Muted (`#EEEEE7`)**: Secondary surface tier for product backdrop canvases, hero underlays, and subtle container blocks.

## Typography

The typography balances high-fashion drama with meticulous transactional utility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`)**: Dramatic high-contrast serifs lend editorial prestige to banners, category headers, and campaign narratives. German characters (Ä, Ö, Ü, ß) retain clear, elegant diacritic positioning.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, contemporary grotesque sans-serif with open apertures and neutral proportions. Ensures crisp legibility across German compound nouns, specifications, and mobile viewports.
- **Price Formatting**: Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold (`€ 49,95`) with non-breaking spaces between symbol and numerical value to preserve visual cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to an architectural rhythm prioritizing breathing room and disciplined alignment:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid system bounded inside a maximum width of `1440px`. Gutters sit at `1.5rem` (`24px`), outer canvas margins at `3rem` (`48px`). Category and product listings utilize balanced 4-column modules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Product listings collapse to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) margins. Products collapse to dense 2-column grids with synchronized heights.
- **Vertical Spacing**: Generous section separation (`space-3xl` / `96px` desktop, `space-2xl` / `64px` mobile) allows photography and editorial typography to stand without visual noise.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is delivered entirely through tectonic surface nesting and structural hairlines rather than simulated shadows:

- **Zero-Shadow Philosophy**: No dropshadows or blur filters are applied to cards, buttons, or dialogs. Visual lift is achieved strictly via surface tint differentiation (`#F8F8F3` against `#EEEEE7`).
- **Precision Hairlines**: Elements are separated by 1px solid borders in `#DCDED5`.
- **Micro-Elevation**: For floating controls (e.g., sticky header on scroll, active size flyouts), depth is indicated by a 1px border stroke (`#1F4A39` or `#DCDED5`) and full opacity fill, preserving an architectural aesthetic.

## Shapes

The design system enforces strict, zero-radius geometry (`roundedness: 0`):

- **Razor Edges**: Every container, card, input field, modal, image frame, swatch, and button features pure `0px` border radii.
- **Editorial Intent**: The crisp right angles reinforce architectural discipline, editorial precision, and contemporary tailoring, letting silhouette photography take center stage.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA (`.btn-primary`)**: Solid deep forest green background (`#1F4A39`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0px` border radius, uppercase tracked typography (`label-md`). Height `48px`, padding `0 28px`. Hover: darken to `#143528`. Focus-visible: 2px solid outline offset by 2px in `#1F4A39`.
- **Secondary / Outline Action (`.btn-outline`)**: Transparent background, 1px solid border (`#171E19` or `#DCDED5`), text in `#171E19`. Height `36px` to `44px`, padding `0 20px`. Hover: `#171E19` fill with `#F8F8F3` text.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Razor-edged box enclosed by a 1px `#DCDED5` hairline border or framed borderless on `#EEEEE7` background.
- **Image Module**: Aspect ratio 4:5 or 3:4 showcasing studio model photography.
- **Content Hierarchy**: Brand label (`label-sm`, `#626960`), Product title (`title-md`, `#171E19`), Price (`price-md`, `#171E19`).
- **Color Swatches**: Strict square swatches (`14px × 14px`), 1px outline in `#DCDED5`. Active swatch features a 1px gap and a 1px outer `#171E19` indicator frame.
- **Action**: Compact outline button ("Ansehen") right-aligned with the swatch rail.

### Category Showcase
- **Module**: 4-column responsive grid.
- **Visuals**: Zero-radius photographic containers on muted `#EEEEE7` base surfaces.
- **Navigation Anchor**: Bottom text row featuring category name (`title-md`) accompanied by a minimal directional arrow (`→`), shifting 4px rightward on hover.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: 1px solid `#DCDED5` outline, `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F8F3` fill, `0px` radius, 44px minimum touch height, `body-md` typography. Focus transitions border to `#1F4A39`.
- **Size Selectors**: Square selector tiles (`40px × 40px`). In stock: 1px `#DCDED5` border, text `#171E19`. Selected: 1.5px `#1F4A39` border with `#1F4A39` text. Unavailable: diagonal hairline strikethrough, `#A0A59D` text, disabled cursor.

### Header & Navigation
- **Top Utility Bar**: Solid `#183F30` background, centered brand roll (`MANSTORE · Olaf Benz · CALIDA`), white `label-sm` typography.
- **Main Bar**: Height 72px, warm white `#F8F8F3` background, 1px bottom border in `#DCDED5`. Left: Brand wordmark paired with high-contrast subtitle "Herrenunterwäsche & Bademode". Center: Minimal uppercase nav anchors ("Neuheiten", "Unterwäsche", "Bademode", "Marken", "Sale"). Right: Slim 1.5px outline stroke utility icons (search, profile, wishlist, bag).
